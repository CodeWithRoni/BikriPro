Everything a garments shop needs

Built around how Bangladeshi shops actually work — cash, bKash, Nagad, credit customers and Eid rush.

🛒

Fast POS

Barcode scan, hold & recall bills, favourites, split payments, waive loose change, print 58/80 mm receipts.

📅

Credit sales & reminders

Pick the next payment date (3 days, 7 days, 1 month). The dashboard reminds you the day it's due.

👕

Size & colour stock

Variants with SKU and barcode, low-stock alerts, and product photos auto-fitted to 500×500.

📒

Customer & supplier ledgers

Separate ledgers with date and type filters, round-off waivers, supplier rebates and commissions.

💳

Cash, bank & MFS

Cash drawer, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, cards and banks — balances, transfers, expenses and statements.

📈

Real profit

Net sales, cost of goods, gross margin and net profit by day and by category.

🏷️

Barcode labels

Load the last purchase, choose what to print and print on A4 sheets or any thermal label size.

🎁

Loyalty & SMS

Points per Taka spent, thank-you SMS after every purchase and automatic birthday wishes.

🛡️

Safe & controlled

Admin / Manager / Cashier roles, activity log, one-click backup and PIN-protected reset.

Know what's due, every morning

Open the dashboard and see who owes you today, how much you sold, your stock value and what to restock.

  • Due collection reminders with days overdue
  • Today's sales, profit and collections
  • Receivables, payables and stock value
  • Best sellers, low stock and birthdays
  • Light and dark mode

Labels in a minute

Pick a recent purchase and every item with its quantity is ready to print.

  • Shop logo, name, product, brand, size/colour, SKU, MRP — toggle each
  • A4 sheets: 24, 40 or 65 labels per page
  • Thermal presets or any custom size in mm
  • Live preview before printing
